Designed from the room outward

Good decisions happen
before the first cut.

Scale, use, material, sightlines, access, and existing conditions all shape the right solution.

Tim helps define what the piece needs to do, how it should relate to nearby finishes, and where the project needs flexibility. That thinking creates a clearer scope and a more coherent result.

  • Storage and everyday function
  • Proportion, alignment, and sightlines
  • Wood species, grain, color, and finish
  • Hardware, lighting, and trade coordination
  • Installation access and site protection
